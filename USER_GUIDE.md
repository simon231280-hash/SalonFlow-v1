# USER GUIDE

# SalonFlow User Guide

Welcome to SalonFlow.

This guide explains the basic daily workflow for salon staff.

---

# Login

1. Open SalonFlow in your web browser.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Click **Login**.

After logging in, you will be taken to the Dashboard.

---

# Dashboard

The Dashboard provides an overview of:

* Customers
* Employees
* Products
* Inventory
* Today's appointments
* Sales summary

---

# Customer Management

Open **Customers**.

You can:

* Add a new customer
* Edit customer information
* Delete customers
* Search customers

Always create a customer before booking an appointment.

---

# Employee Management

Open **Employees**.

You can:

* Add employees
* Update employee information
* Remove employees

---

# Service Management

Open **Services**.

You can:

* Add salon services
* Set service prices
* Set service duration

Examples:

* Hair Cut
* Hair Wash
* Hair Color
* Facial

---

# Product Management

Open **Products**.

You can:

* Add products
* Update prices
* Track stock quantity

---

# Inventory

Open **Inventory**.

Functions:

* Stock In
* Stock Out
* Inventory Adjustment

Always update stock whenever products are received.

---

# Appointment Booking

Open **Appointments**.

To create an appointment:

1. Select a customer.
2. Select an employee.
3. Select one or more services.
4. Choose appointment time.
5. Save.

SalonFlow automatically checks for scheduling conflicts.

---

# Calendar

Open **Calendar**.

Features:

* View appointments by day
* Move to previous or next day
* Select any date
* Click an appointment to edit it
* View services
* View assigned employee
* View appointment status

---

# Checkout

Open **Checkout**.

Process:

1. Select an appointment.
2. Review services.
3. Confirm payment.
4. Complete checkout.

Stock will be updated automatically when payment is completed.

---

# Invoices

Open **Invoices**.

You can:

* View invoices
* Review payment status
* View invoice details

---

# Reports

Open **Reports**.

Available reports include:

* Sales Report
* Employee Sales
* Product Sales
* Service Sales
* Customer Reports
* Inventory Reports

---

# Settings

Open **Settings**.

Update:

* Salon information
* Address
* Phone number
* Currency
* Tax rate
* Receipt footer

---

# Logout

Click **Logout** when finished using the system.

This protects customer and business information.

---

# Best Practices

* Back up the database regularly.
* Do not share login credentials.
* Verify inventory after deliveries.
* Confirm appointment details with customers.
* Review reports at the end of each day.

Thank you for using SalonFlow.
